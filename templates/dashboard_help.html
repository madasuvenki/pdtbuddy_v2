Dashboard Overview
KPI tiles, milestones, MTBF snapshot and CR Age indicator for every target.
What you see
KPI Tiles
Total CRs, Open CRs, Mapped JIRAs, Closed JIRAs. Click any tile to open the full detail table for that category.
Milestones
ES / FC / CS / CS1 dates and MTBF goals. Green = on track, Amber = at risk. Admins can edit from the milestone button.
MTBF Snapshot
Latest MTBF per META as a bar chart. Use the CRM / ENG toggle to switch between customer-reported and engineering build views.
CR Age (Overview)
CR Age = days from CR creation date to today. Shown as a quick indicator of how long open CRs have been waiting. Calculated from the weekly CSV imported every Monday.
Use the CRM / ENG toggle (top right) to switch views across all charts and tables on the page.
🔍
Search Box
Jump directly to any CR or JIRA ticket from anywhere in PDT Buddy.
What you can search
CR Number
Type a CR number e.g. CR4483004. The box turns blue with a CR badge. Press Go or Enter to open the full CR Info page with all linked JIRAs, area, status, devices hit and more.
JIRA Ticket
Type a JIRA key e.g. QSTABILITY-1234 or CHIPMD-5678. The box turns purple. Press Go to open the JIRA detail page showing linked CR, device serial, team, MCN and build info.
What the CR Info page shows
Quick Summary
Total linked JIRAs, occurrences, devices hit, latest META seen — all at a glance at the top.
Identity
CR number, mapped CR group, JIRA link, latest META, sibling CRs, test teams, MCN types, device IDs.
Classification
Status badge, area, functionality, subsystem, full title, CR creation date, build details.
JIRAs Table
First 20 linked JIRAs with filters for serial, team, MCN. Each ticket links to the JIRA tracker.
Click any Linked CR pill on the CR Info page to jump to a sibling CR in the same mapped group.

Weekly Report (Target)
Per-target weekly CR and JIRA metrics with CR Age bar chart and CR Area pie chart.
CR Age Bar Chart
1
Shows open CRs grouped into age buckets: 0–20, 21–40, 41–60, 61–90, 90+ days. Each bar = count of CRs in that age range.
2
CR Age = days from CR creation date to today. Creation date comes from the weekly CSV imported every Monday.
3
A tall bar in the 90+ bucket means many long-standing unresolved CRs — good candidates for escalation in reviews.
CR Area Pie Chart
1
Shows distribution of CRs by Tech Area (Camera, Modem, Core, Display etc.) for the selected date range.
2
Use the CR Status / CR Area toggle to switch the pie between status breakdown and area breakdown.
3
Click a slice to highlight it. Click a legend item to show/hide that slice. Drag labels to reposition — positions are saved per mode.
4
Click Copy Table for an Outlook-friendly HTML snippet. Click Copy Chart to copy the pie as a PNG image.
Date Range & Filters
1
Pick a From / To date range — the summary table and charts update to show only CRs and JIRAs in that period.
2
Use Category filters (checkboxes) to include or exclude specific statuses or areas from the pie chart.

BU Weekly Reports
Cross-target weekly summary — 5 cards each clickable for full detail.
The 5 Cards
📈 CR Age
Bar chart of open CRs by age bucket (0–20, 21–40, 41–60, 61–90, 90+ days) plus hero tiles: Total Unique CRs, New CRs (first seen this week), Old CRs (carried over). Click to see the full CR list sorted by age.
🏳 CR Area Pie
Pie chart of all CRs split by Tech Area across all BU targets for the selected week. Click to see the full area breakdown table.
🔗 SharePoint Data
CRs manually tracked in your team’s SharePoint sheet for this week. Shows CR key and title. Click to see the full list with search and copy.
📊 Unique Weekly Report
Tiles showing Total, QIPL, SD, CH unique CR counts for the week and the source Excel file used. Click to see the full unique CR table.
🏭 Farm Testing
Opens the Farm Testing dashboard in a new tab. Quick link to the internal farm status page.
How to use
1
Click Weekly Reports in the left sidebar to open the BU Weekly Reports page.
2
Use the week dropdown (top left) to select a different week. Data is grouped Monday–Sunday.
3
Click any card to open its full detail page with a searchable, copyable table.
4
Use Import Latest CSV to pull the latest QIPL weekly file from the shared folder, or Upload / Select File to manually upload an Excel or CSV.

SharePoint CRs
CRs manually tracked in your team’s SharePoint sheet — shown in BU Weekly Reports.
What is it?

The SharePoint Data card in BU Weekly Reports shows CRs that your team has manually added to a SharePoint tracking sheet for the selected week. These are CRs your team is actively monitoring — separate from the automated JIRA ingest flow.

What it shows
1
Each row shows a CR key and title. The badge on the card shows the total count for the week.
2
Click the card to open the full detail page. Use Search to filter by CR ID or title.
3
Click Copy Table to copy the list for pasting into emails or weekly reports.
If the card shows “No sharepoint data for this week”, no CRs were added to the SharePoint sheet for that week.

Unique CRs
All unique CRs observed on this target — Venn diagram, area breakdown and full CR table.
What is a Unique CR?

A Unique CR is a CR observed at least once on this target’s PDT devices. The same CR is counted only once regardless of how many JIRAs or devices it appeared on. Data comes from all JIRA tickets linked to this target, de-duplicated by CR number. Refreshed nightly.

Venn Diagram — CR Distribution
PDT Unique
CRs that only your PDT team has hit. No other team has reported this CR. Highest priority to investigate and drive to resolution.
Overlapping
CRs that both your PDT team and other teams have hit. Shared issues already tracked by multiple teams.
Other Teams
CRs reported by other teams but not yet seen on your PDT devices. Awareness only.
Click legend items
Click a Venn legend item to filter the breakdown chart below to only that category.
CR Breakdown Chart
1
Use By Area / By Subsystem / By Functionality dropdown to change the grouping dimension.
2
Use Top 5 / Top 10 / All buttons to control how many bars are shown.
3
Click Copy to copy chart data as tab-separated text for pasting into Excel.
Unique CRs Tab (Full Table)
1
Click the Unique CRs tab in the target dashboard to open the full sortable table of all unique CRs.
2
Columns: CR ID, Title, Area, Subsystem, Status, CR Age (days), JIRA count, first seen META, last seen META.
3
Use Search to filter by CR ID or title. Use Area / Status dropdowns to narrow down. Click any CR ID to open its full CR Info page.
4
Click Copy Table to export the filtered list for reports or emails.

HWPDT Summary
Hardware PDT build table — crashes per META with CHIPMD ticket links.
What is HWPDT?

HWPDT (Hardware PDT) tracks hardware-related crashes per META build. Unlike SW PDT which tracks software crashes via JIRA/QStability, HWPDT tracks hardware failures reported via CHIPMD tickets.

What the table shows
META ID
The metabuild group. Expand a META row to see the individual builds that contributed crashes.
HW Crashes
Total hardware crashes for that META. Only METAs with at least one crash are shown.
CHIPMD Ticket
The linked CHIPMD JIRA ticket for the hardware failure. Click to open in the JIRA tracker.
Mode
CRM or ENG — matches the toggle mode selected on the dashboard.
HWPDT tab only appears if hardware PDT data is available for this target.

Open CR Analysis
Track and annotate Open / Analysis CRs with reproduction scenarios and tech notes.
What to keep here

Open CR Analysis is your team’s working notes board for CRs currently in Open or Analysis state. Use it to record reproduction scenarios, debug findings, and technical observations so the whole team has context in one place — no separate spreadsheet needed.

What info it loads
Auto-loaded CRs
On page open, all CRs that already have saved notes are loaded automatically. Your previous work is always visible without searching.
CR Details
For each CR: CR ID (links to CR Info), title, area, subsystem, status badge, JIRA count, latest META seen, and CR Age in days.
Scenarios
Free-text field for reproduction steps — how to trigger the crash, which device, which build, what conditions.
Tech Notes
Free-text field for debug findings — log analysis, suspected root cause, workarounds, links to related tickets.
How to use
1
Use the Search box to find a CR by ID or title, then select it from the dropdown to add it to the table.
2
Click the Scenarios cell and type reproduction steps. Cell turns amber when there are unsaved changes.
3
Click the Tech Notes cell to add debug findings and observations.
4
Click Save All Notes (green button) to save everything at once. Notes persist across sessions for all team members.
Use Area and Status filters to narrow down to specific CRs for focused reviews. Count badge updates live.

MTBF Table
Edit builds, hours and crashes; manage JIRA exclusions per META.
View Mode (default)
1
All fields are read-only. SWPDT Crashes shows as a blue link — click to open the JIRA list for that META.
2
Use Show last N metas or the Select metas panel to focus on specific META IDs.
Edit Mode
1
Click Edit — checkboxes, hours, crashes, MTBF and MODE become editable.
2
Tick/untick builds — only ticked builds contribute crashes to the META total.
3
Exclude JIRAs — click the button on a META row to open a popup. Uncheck tickets to subtract their crashes from the total.
4
Click Save to persist all changes, or Cancel to discard. Use Reset to Auto to clear all manual overrides.

Live Status
Share real-time PDT execution crash status with external teams during an active Axiom run.
What is Live Status?

After submitting an Axiom job and waiting 10–15 minutes for it to start, you create a Live Status to share the real-time crash count with external stakeholders. Crashes update automatically every 15 minutes from the running job. You choose whether to show Hours and MTBF or just the crash count.

CRM Build vs ENG Build
CRM Build
You enter Hours as input. MTBF is calculated continuously as the job runs. Hours reduce automatically based on the reduction % you set. MTBF chart updates cumulatively.
ENG Build
You do not enter Hours. No MTBF is calculated or shown. Only crash count is tracked and shared. ENG builds do not require MTBF reporting.
How Hours & Reduction Work (CRM only)
1
Enter your starting hours (e.g. 100). This is the total device-hours available for the run.
2
Select a Reduction % per minute per device. Example: 20% means 12 seconds out of every 60 seconds per device are subtracted (20% × 60 sec = 12 sec).
3
PDT Buddy auto-calculates running hours based on your published time and the reduction %. No manual update needed as time passes.
4
MTBF = Running Hours ÷ Crash Count. Updates every 15 minutes when crashes refresh.
Example: 20% reduction → 12 sec removed per 60 sec per device.
10 devices × 1 hour = 10 hrs raw → after 20% reduction = 8 hrs effective → MTBF = 8 ÷ crashes
Saving & Starting a New CRM Job
1
When the current job ends and you want to start a new CRM job, first Revoke the existing live status.
2
Before revoking, click Save MTBF & Hours. You can edit the values at this point before saving.
3
Saved Hours and Crashes are written to the Excel file for this target. The MTBF chart picks up these values.
4
The MTBF chart is cumulative — it adds saved values from each completed job to show the overall MTBF trend across all runs.
5
After saving and revoking, create a new Live Status for the next Axiom job.
Toggle Show Hours & MTBF on/off to control what external viewers see. Turn off to share only crash count without MTBF.

Target Compare Studio
Compare CR data side-by-side across multiple targets.
Getting started
1
Click Target Delta in the top bar. Type target names and select 2 or more to compare.
2
Optionally set CR Status filters and date range, then click Run Compare.
Result Tabs
CR by Tech Area
Bar chart + table of CR counts per area for each target. Select an area to drill into subsystem level.
Common CRs
CRs appearing in ALL selected targets — shared issues across platforms.
Exclusive CRs
CRs unique to only one target — platform-specific issues.
Regression CRs
CRs marked as regression across the compared targets.
Use Copy Table and Copy Chart to paste results into PowerPoint or email.

Device Summary
SW & HW PDT device counts and deployment table — managed via Excel upload.
Setup (Admin)
1
Click Upload Excel and select your .xlsx file. PDT Buddy stores a managed copy.
2
PDT Buddy auto-detects the Devices sheet. Select the correct sheet if needed, then click Save Config.
3
No template needed — click Create Managed Excel to generate a blank file with correct headers.
Excel Sheet Structure
SW PDT Summary Sheet
Deployment counts. Columns: Form Factor | MCN | Storage then per site (QIPL, CH, SD): Delivered | Deployed and a Total column.
Devices Sheet
Full device list. One row per device. Key columns: Serial, MCN, DDR Type, Memory bundle, Storage, Platform, Station, Location, Rework details, Comments.
What you see
Deployment Table
Rows by Form Factor / MCN / Storage. Columns per site showing Delivered and Deployed counts.
MCN Chart
Bar chart of delivered device count per MCN variant.
Device List
Full table from the Devices sheet. Filter by Serial, MCN, Location. Click Export CSV to download.
Add Device
Click Add Device to add a row directly in PDT Buddy. Saved back to the managed Excel automatically.

Buddy Assistant
AI-powered right-side drawer — ask questions or run JIRA filter reports.
How to open
1
Click the Buddy button (bottom-right of any page). First-time users enter a QGenie API key once — saved for the session.
2
Ask in plain English. Responses may include links, tables, or charts. Click Copy to copy output. Use Reset to start fresh.
Example Questions
CR Queries
show me open CRs for camera how many built CRs do we have? find crash CRs in modem show details for CR4483004 what are the related CRs for CR123456?
MTBF & Analysis
show MTBF trend for last 3 metas top 5 open CRs for modem area count of open vs built CRs which meta had the most crashes?
JiraQuery Filter Report
jiraquery="filter=115827" $processCRinfo JiraQueryLimit=2000 $SkipHwPdtQStabilityCheck
filter=115827
Replace with your own JIRA saved filter ID.
$processCRinfo
Enriches each JIRA result with CR data from the PDT database.
JiraQueryLimit=2000
Max rows to fetch. Default 1000. Increase for large filters.
$SkipHwPdtQStabilityCheck
Skip HW PDT QStability validation. Use for non-HW PDT filters.

Navigation & User Roles
How to move around PDT Buddy and what each role can do.
Page Structure
Left Sidebar — BU List
Click any Business Unit (Mobile, Compute, XR etc.) to see its targets.
Target Tabs
Inside a target: Dashboard, Unique CRs, PDT CRs, Open JIRAs, Weekly Data, Open CR Analysis, MTBF Table, HWPDT, Device Summary tabs.
Top Bar
BU / Target dropdowns for quick switching. Target Delta opens compare studio.
Customize Tabs
Click the settings icon on the tab bar to show/hide specific tabs. Hidden tabs also skip data loading for faster page loads.
User Roles
Full Access
PDT group members. Can view all dashboards, edit MTBF tables, save notes, and use all features.
Viewer
Read-only. Can view all dashboards and charts but cannot edit, save, or access admin features.
Admin
Full access plus Admin Panel — add/update targets, manage milestones, set page visibility, manage user privileges.
Dark Mode
Toggle from the bottom of the left sidebar. Preference saved across sessions.
If a section is missing (e.g. Unique CRs tab), it may be hidden by your admin via Page Visibility settings.
