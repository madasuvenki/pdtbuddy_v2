**Development Team Insights:**
The IPA uC (microcontroller) is receiving a garbage uCP command and packet context, resulting in no response to the uC command, which causes a timeout and subsequent kernel assert. **Root Cause Analysis:**
The APPS kernel crash is triggered by a uC command timeout in `ipa3_uc_send_cmd_64b_param` at line 1165, which calls `ipa_assert` leading to a kernel BUG at `ipa.c:286`. The IPA microcontroller receives a garbage uCP command and packet context during USB xDCI channel bring-up (via `ipa3_xdci_start` → `ipa3_start_gsi_debug_monitor` → `ipa3_uc_debug_stats_alloc`), and fails to send a response within the timeout window. The IU registers being all 0s at crash time suggests the uC itself may have crashed or been reset prior to the APPS-side timeout. A similar root cause was identified in DROIDBUG-15704114, where a fatal IPA hardware IRQ originating on the modem side propagated to trigger the APPS assert. The exact trigger for the garbage command/context corruption is still under investigation by the IPA UC team.
